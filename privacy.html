Privacy and data use

What Family Organizer does with health records it reads from MyChart.

Who runs it

A single family, on its own server at home, for its own members. There is no company behind it, no advertising, no fees and no outside funding.

What is collected

Only what the patient allows in MyChart: their lab results and visits. Nothing is read without their sign-in and approval.

Where it is kept

  • On the family's own server. Records are encrypted with a key that only the patient's journal unlocks (by their PIN, Face ID or printed recovery key). Other family members, including whoever runs the server, cannot read them.
  • The patient may choose to share their journal, read-only, with family members they pick, one at a time; they can stop sharing at any time. They get a notification each time someone opens it (they can turn this off), and every opening is listed in the journal.

What is never done

  • Records are never sold, shared with anyone outside the patient's own choice, or used for advertising or research.
  • Records are never sent to any other company or service.
  • The app never writes to or changes the medical record.

Keeping and deleting

Records stay in the patient's journal until the patient deletes them. Any entry can be deleted at any time, and Delete my journal erases the whole journal at once, including everything imported from MyChart, and forgets the MyChart connection. Removing someone from the family's app erases their journal straight away. The family server's nightly backups keep a locked copy for up to two weeks before it is gone; it cannot be opened without the patient's own PIN or recovery key. Removing the app in MyChart (linked apps) ends its access at the health system.

Access log

The journal lists every time someone the patient shares it with opens it. The app also notes when MyChart was last read and how many items came back, never their content.

Family Organizer is a private app run by one family on its own server. It is not a commercial product and is not affiliated with Epic, MyChart or any health system.
