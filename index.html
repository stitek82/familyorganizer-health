Family Organizer: health records

How the Family Organizer app connects to MyChart.

What it is

Family Organizer is a private, self-hosted app used by one family. It runs on the family's own server and is only reachable by the family's own devices. Part of it is a personal health journal, where a family member keeps track of symptoms, medications, lab results and doctor visits for themselves.

What the MyChart connection does

With the patient's permission, the app reads their lab results and visits from their health system's MyChart (through the standard SMART on FHIR interface) and adds them to their own health journal, so they don't have to be typed in by hand. It only reads; it never changes anything in the medical record.

How it works

  • The patient opens Health records in their journal, picks their health system and signs in on the health system's own MyChart page. The app never sees their MyChart user name or password.
  • MyChart shows exactly what the app may read, and for how long, and asks the patient to allow it.
  • What is read is encrypted straight away with a key only the patient's journal can open, and kept on the family's server.
  • The patient can remove the app's access at any time in MyChart (linked apps), and disconnect it in the journal.

Data read

Lab results (Observation), visits (Encounter) and the patient's identity (Patient), read-only.

Questions

Ask the person who runs your family's Family Organizer server.
